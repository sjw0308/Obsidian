
Generic Signal Energy and Power
- Total energy of continuous signal x(t) over $[t_{1}, t_{2}]$
	- $E=\int_{t_{1}}^{t_{2}}|x(t)|^2dt$
	- $x = a+bj = r\cos\phi+jr\sin\phi = re^{\phi j}$
- For Discrete over $[n_1, n_{2}]$
	- $E=\sum^{n_{2}}_{n=n_{1}}|x[n]|^2$
- Average Power P
	- 에너지를 기간으로 나눈 값
- Infinite time($-\infty,\infty$)에서...
	- Finite total energy: zero average power
	- Finite average power: infinite total energy

- Time Shift Signal Transformation
	- $y(t)=x(at+b)$
